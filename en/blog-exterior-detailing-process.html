Exterior Detailing: The Safe Process for a Perfect Look

Snow foam, two-bucket wash, decontamination and protection — exterior detailing step by step in a premium studio.

Exterior detailing is the foundation of every premium car care routine. Unlike a quick wash, the process is designed to clean the vehicle without creating new paint scratches.

The first step is always snow foam — thick foam that softens dirt and lifts it from the paint before any brush or mitt contact.

Two-bucket wash method

One bucket with clean water, one with shampoo. The mitt is rinsed in clean water before every re-dip — the gold standard for safe washing.

Decontamination

Iron fallout remover for brake dust particles, clay bar for bonded contaminants and tar remover for asphalt. This prepares paint for polishing or ceramic coating.
