Privacy & cookies

This is a static student edition for FINKI Business Practice. It contains no analytics, advertising scripts, embedded maps or message collection server.

Local storage

dolphin-consent-v1 stores your choice for up to 180 days in this browser. If preferences are allowed, dolphin-theme remembers your theme. Reopen settings in the footer to change your choice. Necessary only removes the saved theme. The page URL determines the language.

Contact form

Form data stays in the open page’s memory. No network submission takes place. Downloading creates a text file on your device; refreshing clears the form. External social and map links open those services only when clicked and their privacy policies apply.
