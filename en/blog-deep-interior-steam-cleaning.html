Deep Interior Cleaning with Steam

Steam is effective, chemical-free and deep — ideal for upholstery, carpets and hard-to-reach areas.

Steam cleaning uses high temperature to break down dirt, bacteria and odours — without aggressive chemicals. Ideal for family cars, pet owners or allergy sufferers.

What gets steam cleaned?

Seat upholstery, carpets, headliner, vent grilles, gaps between seats and hard-to-reach corners. Steam also removes bacteria from the A/C system.

Combined with extraction

For best results, steam is combined with extraction — steam loosens dirt, the extractor pulls it from deep within.
