How to Maintain Your Ceramic Coating

Ceramic coating isn ' t ' set and forget ' . Here ' s what to do to make it last 2–5 years.

Ceramic coating significantly reduces maintenance but doesn ' t eliminate it. Proper care extends durability and keeps hydrophobicity strong.

Washing rules

Wash cool and in shade. Use pH-neutral wax-free shampoo. Two-bucket method. Avoid automatic washes. Dry with microfibre to prevent water spots.

Spray за одржување

Every 3–6 months apply a ceramic boost or maintenance spray — this renews the hydrophobic layer and reinforces protection.
