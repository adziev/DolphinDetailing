Professional Detailing vs. Automatic Car Wash

Automatic washes are fast but often damage paint. Here ' s why professional detailing is the better investment.

Automatic car washes use rotating brushes and high pressure that create micro-scratches — swirls visible under LED light. Over time, paint loses gloss and depth.

Professional detailing uses safe techniques, pH-balanced products and a controlled environment — like our studio with hexagonal LED lighting.

Cost comparison

Automatic wash: cheap, but paint gets damaged. Paint correction: expensive. Professional wash + protection: a reasonable investment that extends paint life.

When is an automatic wash acceptable?

Only if the car is very dirty and you have no other option — and even then, rarely. For any vehicle with value or emotional attachment, professional care is the only right choice.
